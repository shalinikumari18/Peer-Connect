Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common doubts

What is peer tutoring?
Students helping other students learn a subject.
Do I have to pay?
No. Peer-Connect is student-powered and free to join.
Can I be both a tutor and a learner?
Yes! That is Skill Exchange: teach one subject, learn another.
How do I request a session?
Open Find Tutors, choose a tutor and click Request, then fill in the form.
How are accounts kept safe?
Students join with a college email, and can report or block anyone.
How do I earn XP?
By learning, teaching, helping others and keeping daily streaks.

Still stuck? Join or send a request.
