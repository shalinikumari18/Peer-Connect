About Peer-Connect

Learn from students. Teach other students.

Peer tutoring is when a student helps another student learn a subject. Because tutors are students too, they understand your difficulties and explain things in a friendly way. Everyone benefits: learners get help and tutors strengthen their own knowledge.

Signature Feature: Skill Exchange

Nobody is only a tutor or only a learner

A student can teach Python to another student and, in return, learn Mathematics from them. You switch roles based on what you know and what you want to learn.
